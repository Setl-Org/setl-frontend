## Direction contract

THESIS: A Plaid-shaped landing whose hero is a working Setl connect flow, lit by the Setl mark. Pick a bank, approve, and see the JSON an app receives. It refuses the template hero, invented scale, and customer logos.

OWN-WORLD: A white primary hero (user pin), then full-bleed fields from deep plum #1d0b30 through Setl purple #542774 to magenta #8e3a83 and #c44fa3, textured with line-art from the mark's hatching. Lilac #F3EEF9 paper sections and selection fills; ink #141414 text. Floating white product screens with soft plum shadows; pill controls (ink on white, white on colour); real institution logos, unblurred; Schibsted Grotesk display, tight tracking; 28–36px radii.

STORY: The visitor reads "the financial data layer for Pakistan", runs the example connect flow, sees the shape their app gets, scrolls use cases shown as product screens, learns the honest current state (statements today, banks as they join), then talks to the team or gets API keys.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White hero under a floating white pill nav (user pinned white primary, 2026-11-11). Left: an ink headline at about 84px, subcopy, and two equal pills, "Talk to the team" (ink solid) and "Get API keys" (white, ink ring), plus an NIC Karachi chip. Right: the working three-step connect sheet as frosted glass floating on white, no stage panel (user decisions 2026-11-11), with the half Setl mark in pale lilac line-art radiating from behind it to the right edge. The hero ends in a row of four product truths.

FORM: Plaid marketing canon (plaid.com, 2026).
